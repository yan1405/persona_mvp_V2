Circular avatar for people and for the Agente Persona mark; photos are the only imagery in product.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Yan" />
<Avatar variant="ai" name="Agente Persona" size={32} />
```

- `variant="ai"` is reserved for the Agente Persona (AgentPanel, MessageBubble) and must always sit next to the visible label "Agente Persona".
